    %% Deposit Flow
    User -->|1. Deposit USDC| DepositIX
    DepositIX -->|Issue Shares| ShareMint
    ShareMint -.->|Mint LP Tokens| User
    DepositIX -->|Transfer USDC| USDCVault

    %% Keeper & Rebalance Flow
    PythOracle -.->|Fetch Price Feeds| Keeper
    Keeper -->|2. Trigger Dynamic Rebalance| RebalanceIX
    RebalanceIX -->|Check Feeds: CI / P < 0.5%| PythOracle
    RebalanceIX -->|Verify Authority Signature| VaultAuthority

    %% CPI Routing
    RebalanceIX -->|3a. Deposit USDC Collateral| MF_USDCLend
    RebalanceIX -->|3b. Borrow SOL against USDC| MF_SOLBorrow
    MF_SOLBorrow -->|Disburse SOL| VaultAuthority
    VaultAuthority -->|3c. Deposit Borrowed SOL into Yield Reserve| KM_Collateral
    KM_Collateral -->|Yield Accrues| KM_Obligation

    %% Harvest & Compounding
    Keeper -->|4. Crank Yield Harvest| HarvestIX
    HarvestIX -->|Claim Kamino LP/Supply Rewards| KM_Collateral
    HarvestIX -->|Pay Marginfi Borrow Interest| MF_SOLBorrow
    HarvestIX -->|Surplus USDC Reinvested| USDCVault

    %% Withdrawal Flow
    User -->|5. Burn LP Shares| WithdrawIX
    WithdrawIX -->|Unwind Kamino Position| KM_Collateral
    WithdrawIX -->|Repay SOL Borrow| MF_SOLBorrow
    WithdrawIX -->|Withdraw USDC Collateral| MF_USDCLend
    WithdrawIX -->|Return Net USDC| User
